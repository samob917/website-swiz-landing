# Blog fact-check: ACGME claims

The 48 posts in Wisp were written by an outside SEO contractor and never
checked against the Common Program Requirements. Every specific regulatory
claim they make is listed below, so verification is a checklist rather than a
re-read of 120,000 words.

Checked against the **ACGME Common Program Requirements (Residency) 2026**,
interim revision effective 9 February 2026, Section 6 (Clinical and
Educational Work Hours). Source PDF: acgme.org/globalassets/pfassets/
programrequirements/2026-prs/cprresidency_2026.pdf

**370 claims checked. 57 wrong, across 13 of 48 posts.**

## What the 2026 requirements actually say

| Rule | Requirement | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly maximum | 80 hours, averaged over four weeks, including clinical work from home (EHR, calls from home) | 6.19 |
| Exception | Up to 88 hours, only by Review Committee approval | — |
| Continuous work | Must not exceed 24 hours, plus up to 4 additional hours for transitions of care or education | 6.22, 6.22.a |
| **PGY-1 hour cap** | **None.** No PGY-1-specific limit exists. The 16-hour cap was removed in July 2017 | — |
| PGY-1 restrictions | May not moonlight; not initially permitted at-home call | 6.25.b, FAQ |
| Between work periods | Residents **should** have eight hours off. Advisory, not mandatory | 6.20 |
| **10-hour recommendation** | **Does not exist in the current text.** It was part of the 2011 standards | — |
| After 24h in-house call | At least 14 hours free | 6.21.a |
| Days free | One day in seven, averaged over four weeks | 6.21.b |
| In-house call | No more frequent than every third night | FAQ |

## The four error patterns

1. **Attributing the PGY-1 change to the 2026 revision (26 instances).** There is a
   real 2026 interim revision, so the reference is not invented. But it did not
   remove the PGY-1 16-hour cap. That happened in July 2017. The substance
   ("PGY-1s follow 24+4") is correct; the date attached to it is not.
2. **The PGY-1 16-hour cap stated as current (16).**
3. **A 10-hour rest recommendation (8).** Not in the 2026 text at all; it is from
   the superseded 2011 standards.
4. **The 8-hour break described as mandatory (7).** The wording is "should have",
   which is advisory.
